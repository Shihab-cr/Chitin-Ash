TEXT FOR THE "AI DESCRIPTION" FIELD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tool used: Anthropic Claude (large language model), operating Blender through a
scripting interface.

What the AI produced: Claude wrote the Python scripts that generate the mesh,
the armature, the vertex weights, the UV layout, the three palette textures and
the keyframes of all five animation clips inside Blender. The geometry is
therefore procedurally constructed from numeric parameters in those scripts.

What was NOT used: no generative 3D model service (text-to-3D or image-to-3D),
no diffusion or image-generation model, and no photogrammetry or scanned data.
No mesh, texture or animation in this package was sampled, retopologised or
derived from any existing third-party asset or generated artwork.

Human contribution: the design brief, silhouette, proportions, colour scheme,
Egyptian motif selection and gait behaviour were directed by the publisher, who
reviewed every iteration and requested the corrections that produced the final
result — including reworking the body proportions, the leg joint structure, the
texture and material values, and replacing a whole-body turn with a true
sideways gait. The publisher is responsible for the final topology, rig, weights
and animation timing as shipped.
